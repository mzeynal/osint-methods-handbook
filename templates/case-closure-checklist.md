# Case closure checklist

- [ ] The client's question is answered in the report, with a confidence level
- [ ] A second analyst checked the key conclusions against the source log
- [ ] Legal and ethical review done; right of reply offered where a person or organisation may be harmed
- [ ] Evidence log complete: every file has a hash, a source and a collection time
- [ ] Gaps and limits are written down
- [ ] Personal data not needed any more is deleted; the list of deleted material is saved in `99_closure/`
- [ ] Originals and logs archived read-only with an access list
- [ ] Retention period and deletion date recorded
- [ ] Corrections procedure explained to the client
