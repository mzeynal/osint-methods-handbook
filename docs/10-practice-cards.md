# Part 10. Practice cards: checks with consent and on public data

In these cards the work uses real data and lawful grounds: with the person's consent, on public figures and organisations, on closed public cases. Collecting information on private individuals without a basis is not included (section 1.2). Legal frameworks depend on the country; marks (G) mean it needs checking. The workflows are developed in the companion guide [KYC, EDD and Corporate Intelligence](https://github.com/mzeynal/kyc-edd-guide).
| Practice and essence | Tools | Techniques | What to remember |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A background check with consent.** A full cycle of checking a real person who gave written consent: on yourself or on colleagues who agreed (G) | Company registers (directorships), court and insolvency registers, professional registers and licences, official sanctions lists and licensed PEP data, archives, public profiles | Written consent and purpose; verify every claim in the CV against an independent source; tell namesakes apart; a report with a confidence scale (section 8.3) | Hiring checks are regulated (in the US FCRA, in the EU GDPR); minimum data; a retention period; the person has the right to know the result and to contest it |
| **Checking public executives' biographies.** Comparing what public biographies say with registers and documents (G) | Registers of directors, disclosures to securities regulators, an archive of the company's site (section 2.2), the media | Match positions and dates with registers; find earlier versions of biographies in the archive; check degrees and awards with the issuing organisation | A public role, not private life; tell an innocent discrepancy from forgery; record the date of the check |
| **Identity resolution.** Establishing that records in different lists relate to one person (G, S) | Fuzzy matching (rapidfuzz), transliteration (section 3.1) | Normalisation (case, diacritics, word order); matching on name, date of birth, nationality; thresholds and manual checking of the disputed | Record the threshold and the share of false positives; a name match is not a person; keep the decision on each disputed case |
| **Analysing fraudulent profiles and offers.** Fake recruiters, "investors", romance schemes (G) | Reverse image search; WHOIS and RDAP; certificate logs; an archive; checking the provenance of an image (C2PA, SynthID, section 4.2) | The age of the domain and the account; reuse of photos and texts; mismatch with registers; typical schemes of time pressure and prepayment | Do not make contact and do not pay; do not publish victims' data; report to the platform and the police |
| **Batch screening.** Checking a list of names against sanctions lists and PEP data (S) | Official list files or a licensed screening provider (check the licence of any aggregator); tables; Python | Prepare data (name, date of birth, country); match, assess, decide, record; re-screen on a schedule (section 9.6) | Record the list version and date; do not warn the subject (G); pass disputed cases to a lawyer or compliance |
| **Dossiers on closed public cases.** Real, widely covered cases with court documents (G) | Registers, court documents, archives, financial statements, published consortium databases (section 5.2) | A cutoff date; a chronology of signals; a link table (section 8.3); a memorandum (section 8.5) | Only closed cases and public organisations; no private individuals; do not publish without legal assessment |
| **Rules for declining.** When not to take a task (G) | A checklist: lawful basis, consent, proportionality, data minimisation; consulting a lawyer | Record the reason for the refusal in writing; offer a lawful alternative (with consent, on a public figure, on a closed case) | A refusal is part of professionalism; when in doubt escalate; do not keep data "just in case" |
| **Open competitions and community tasks.** Real Bellingcat challenges, quizzes, OSINT CTFs (S) | Search engines, maps, reverse search, archives; participants' write-ups | Limit the time; read other people's write-ups after your own attempt; record your own route | Do not ask for help in surveillance of a specific person; do not publish personal data |
